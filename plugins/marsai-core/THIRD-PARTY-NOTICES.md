# Third-party credits and licences

Some of the skills in this plugin were adapted from Matt Pocock's skills
(<https://github.com/mattpocock/skills>) and adjusted to this flow:
Linear, work across several repositories, the team's component library and
backend standard.

Derived skills:

- `skills/grill-with-docs/` (from `grilling`, `grill-with-docs` and `domain-modeling`)
- `skills/handoff/` (from `handoff`)
- `skills/wayfinder/` (from `wayfinder`, with the map and the questions in Linear)
- `skills/retro/` (from `retro`)
- `skills/open-pr/BODY.md`, only the "Other views" section (from `pr`, which in turn credits the `show-me` skill by Dex Horthy, HumanLayer)
- `skills/using-marsai/` (the flow-map format, from `ask-matt`)

## pstack

The `marsai-mode` skill takes its shape from `poteto-mode` in pstack by Lauren Tan, version 0.15.24, source commit 1e5c90a4b6f249917bd21fe361b3a2a7deb741b8: https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/1e5c90a4b6f249917bd21fe361b3a2a7deb741b8/pstack/skills/poteto-mode. The ideas taken are one front door for any work, the matched playbook copied into a visible task list, a skipped step kept with its reason, finding out a fact instead of asking for it, and labelling where each claim comes from.
