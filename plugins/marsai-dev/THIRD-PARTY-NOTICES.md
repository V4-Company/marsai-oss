# Third-party credits and licences

Some of the skills in this plugin were adapted from Matt Pocock's skills
(<https://github.com/mattpocock/skills>), translated and adjusted to this flow:
Linear, work across several repositories, design system and backend standard.

Derived skills:

- `skills/prototype/` (from `prototype`; the branch for a flow between services is V4's)
- `skills/to-spec/` and `skills/to-tickets/` (from `to-spec` and `to-tickets`, combined with a product discovery skill of V4's own)
- `skills/implement/` (from `implement`)
- `skills/implement-spec/` (from `implement-spec`)
- `skills/tdd/` (from `tdd`)
- `skills/code-review/` (from `code-review`, combined with an example review bot rubric of six dimensions)
